Serving Cyclists in Kursela Since 1993

Established in 1993, Chandan Cycle Store is a family-run business dedicated to serving the cycling needs of Kursela and the wider Katihar community. Our foundation was built on a simple premise: everyone deserves a reliable ride. We've grown from a humble beginning into a trusted local hub for all things cycling, maintaining the warm, personal touch of a neighborhood shop.

We believe in building lasting relationships with our customers. Whether you're looking for a new bicycle or need expert cycle repair services, our team is here to provide honest advice and quality workmanship. Your safety and satisfaction are our top priorities. If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
